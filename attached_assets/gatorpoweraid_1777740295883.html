ABOUT

01 / 05

Gatorpower-Aid is a San Diego-based producer and DJ operating at the edges of electronic music — where industrial texture meets algorithmic rhythm and pixel-era nostalgia collides with club weight.

Known for dense, hyperlinked DJ sets that treat genre as raw material, their mixes have aired on NTS Radio and continue to resist easy categorization.

Works are constructed using step sequencers, drum machines, and Ableton — building percussion architectures that feel both broken and inevitable.

Booking and demos via contact form.
