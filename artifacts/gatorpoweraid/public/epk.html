Biography

Gatorpower-Aid is a San Diego-based DJ and producer working at the edges of electronic music. Drawing from industrial texture, algorithmic rhythm, and pixel-era nostalgia, their sets resist easy categorization — blending genres with surgical precision across club nights, radio broadcasts, and online streams.

A regular on NTS Radio, Gatorpower-Aid has built a reputation for dense, hyperlinked DJ sets that treat genre as raw material, constructing percussion architectures that feel both broken and inevitable. Works are built using step sequencers, drum machines, and Ableton Live.
